Crashes tell you where people have been hurt — a lagging count that takes years to accumulate. Conflicts are the leading signal: near-misses detected without anyone being hit.

This is the Traffic Conflict Technique, which is established road safety practice and normally requires an observer standing at the corner with a clipboard for days. That is why it is almost never done. Here it runs continuously, for free, on a camera already installed.

A corner accumulating conflicts faster than its crash history predicts is where to spend money before someone is hurt.
